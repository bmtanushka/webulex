When someone searches for "cleaners near me", Google shows a map with a handful of local businesses before any ordinary results. For a cleaning company, appearing there can make the difference between a full diary and a quiet week.

This checklist covers the steps that matter most. You can work through it yourself, or ask us to handle it for you.

## 1. Claim and complete your Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile is the listing that appears on Google Search and Maps.

- Claim and verify the profile.
- Choose the most accurate primary category, such as "House cleaning service" or "Commercial cleaning service", and add secondary categories that fit.
- If you visit customers rather than having them visit you, set a **service area** and hide your home address.
- Add your opening hours, phone number, website and a clear description of your services.
- Upload real photos of your team, vehicles and before-and-after results.

## 2. Make your website match your profile

Google compares your website with your profile. Make sure:

- your business name, phone number and address (if shown) match exactly
- you have a page for each main service, such as regular cleaning, deep cleans, end-of-tenancy and office cleaning
- each page says which areas you cover

## 3. Create pages for the areas you serve

If you work across several towns or suburbs, a short, genuinely useful page for each one helps. Mention the services you offer there, local details such as typical property types, and how to book. Avoid copying the same text with only the place name changed.

## 4. Get listed in relevant directories

Consistent listings on trusted directories help Google confirm your details. Focus on reputable general directories and those specific to your country or trade, and keep your name, address and phone number identical everywhere.

## 5. Ask for reviews after every clean

Reviews are one of the strongest local signals and they persuade customers too.

- Send a short message with a direct review link after each job.
- Reply to every review, positive or negative, politely and promptly.
- Never buy reviews or offer rewards for positive ones; it breaks the platforms' rules.

## 6. Post updates regularly

Google Business Profile lets you publish posts and offers. A short weekly update, such as a before-and-after photo or a seasonal offer, shows customers and Google that you're active.

## 7. Make booking easy

Visibility only helps if people can act on it. Add an online quote form that asks for property size, rooms and frequency, a click-to-call button and WhatsApp chat. If you miss calls while on site, an AI call agent can answer and book for you.

## 8. Track what's working

Set up Google Analytics and call tracking so you can see how many enquiries come from your profile, website and ads. Review it monthly and adjust.
