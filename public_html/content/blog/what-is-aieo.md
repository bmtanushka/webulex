More people now ask an AI assistant for a recommendation instead of scrolling through search results. "Who's a good cleaner in Leeds?" or "Which accountant in Sydney works with sole traders?" might be typed into ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews. If those tools don't know about your business, you're not in the answer.

**AIEO (AI Engine Optimisation)** is the work of making sure they do.

## What AIEO actually means

AI assistants build their answers from information they can find and trust: websites, business listings, reviews, directories and articles that mention you. AIEO improves the quality, clarity and consistency of that information so the assistant can understand:

- who you are and where you work
- what you offer and who it's for
- why you're a credible choice

It's closely related to SEO. Most of the foundations, such as a fast, well-structured website and an accurate Google Business Profile, help with both.

## How it differs from traditional SEO

Traditional SEO aims to rank a page in a list of links. AIEO aims to be **quoted or recommended** inside an answer. That changes what matters:

- **Clear, direct answers.** Pages that explain what you do in plain sentences are easier for an AI to quote than pages full of slogans.
- **Consistency everywhere.** If your opening hours, address or services differ between your website and your listings, the assistant has less reason to trust any of them.
- **Mentions beyond your own site.** Reviews, local directories and articles that name your business all add context.

## Practical steps you can take

### 1. Add structured data to your website

Structured data (schema markup) is a small block of code that tells machines your business name, address, services, opening hours and FAQs in a standard format. It's invisible to visitors but very useful to search engines and AI tools.

### 2. Write answer-focused content

Add a short summary near the top of each service page: what it is, who it's for and what's included. Add FAQs that answer the questions customers actually ask, in full sentences.

### 3. Keep your business details consistent

Check your Google Business Profile, social media pages and directory listings. Your name, address, phone number and services should match your website exactly.

### 4. Earn genuine reviews and mentions

Ask happy customers for reviews, and look for relevant local directories, associations and partners that can list your business.

### 5. Check what AI assistants say about you

Ask a few assistants about your type of business in your area and see whether you appear. Repeat it every few months to track progress.

## What AIEO can't do

No one can control what an AI assistant says, and anyone who promises a guaranteed recommendation is overselling. What you can do is give these tools accurate, consistent and useful information about your business, which improves your chances over time.

## Where to start

If you already invest in SEO, AIEO is a natural next step that builds on the same foundations. Start with structured data, clear service summaries and consistent listings, then build reviews and mentions month by month.
