If you run a small business, you've probably missed a call while you were with a customer, up a ladder or driving between jobs. Some callers leave a message; many simply ring the next business on the list.

An **AI call agent** answers those calls for you.

## What is an AI call agent?

It's a phone assistant powered by AI that can hold a natural conversation. It answers when you can't, understands what the caller needs, and takes the next step: answering a question, booking an appointment or passing the details to you.

It's different from a traditional phone menu ("press 1 for…"). Callers simply say what they want.

## What it can do for a small business

- **Answer every call**, including evenings and weekends.
- **Answer common questions** about your services, areas covered, opening hours and guide prices, using information you approve.
- **Capture lead details** such as name, phone number, address and what the job involves.
- **Book appointments** straight into your calendar.
- **Return missed calls** or follow up on enquiries.
- **Hand over to a person** when the caller needs one, and send you a summary of every call.

## How it works behind the scenes

1. **Your number forwards to the agent** when you don't answer, after hours, or for every call, depending on how you set it up.
2. **The agent is trained on your business**: your services, prices, policies and frequently asked questions.
3. **It connects to your tools**, such as your calendar, CRM and messaging, often through an automation platform like n8n.
4. **After each call** you receive a summary by email, WhatsApp or in your CRM, and the customer can get a confirmation message.

## What to check before you set one up

- **Be open with callers.** It's good practice for the agent to say it's an AI assistant and offer a person when needed.
- **Keep the information accurate.** The agent is only as good as the details you give it. Review them when prices or services change.
- **Think about privacy.** Choose reputable providers, tell customers how their data is used in your privacy policy, and only collect what you need.
- **Plan for edge cases.** Decide what happens with complaints, emergencies or unusual requests, and make sure those go straight to you.
- **Understand the costs.** Most providers charge for call minutes on top of any setup. Ask for these to be shown separately in any quote.

## Is it right for your business?

An AI call agent is most useful when:

- you regularly miss calls because you're busy with customers
- many calls ask the same questions
- bookings follow a predictable pattern

It works best alongside a chatbot on your website and WhatsApp, and a CRM that keeps every lead in one place.
