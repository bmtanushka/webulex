This policy explains how **Webulex Technology & Accounting Solutions (PVT) Ltd** ("Webulex", "we", "us") collects and uses personal information when you visit webulexpro.com, contact us or work with us.

## Who we are

Webulex Technology & Accounting Solutions (PVT) Ltd is a company registered in Sri Lanka (Reg. PV00237882), at 285/6, Sudarshana Mawatha, Malabe 10115, Sri Lanka. We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

For any privacy question or request, email **info@webulexpro.com** or call **+94 77 227 5760**.

## What we collect

**Information you give us**, for example through our contact and consultation forms, by email, phone or WhatsApp, or when you book a call:

- your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number and country
- your business type, the services or packages you're interested in, your budget range and your message
- any details you share during a project

**Information collected automatically:**

- **Essential cookies and security data.** A session cookie that protects our forms against misuse, and a cookie that remembers your cookie choices. To stop spam we keep a one-way (hashed) version of your IP address for up to an hour, and we may use Cloudflare Turnstile to check that a form is sent by a person.
- **Analytics (only with your consent).** If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics 4 collects information about how you use the site, such as pages viewed and the approximate region you visit from.
- **Campaign information.** If you arrive from an ad or email link, we record the campaign tags (UTM tags) with your enquiry.

## How we use it and our legal basis

- **To reply to your enquiry and provide quotes or services:** to take steps at your request before a contract, and to perform our contract with you.
- **To run, secure and improve our website:** our legitimate interests in operating a safe, working website.
- **To understand how our website is used:** your consent (analytics cookies), which you can withdraw at any time.
- **To keep business and tax records:** our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

- **Cloudflare:** spam protection (Turnstile) and, if enabled, website delivery
- **Google:** analytics (only with your consent)
- our **booking calendar** and **chat** providers, if you use them

## International transfers

We are based in Sri Lanka and serve clients in the United States, United Kingdom, Singapore and Australia, and some of our providers operate in other countries. Where we transfer personal information internationally, we take steps to protect it, for example by using providers that offer appropriate contractual safeguards.

## How long we keep it

- Enquiries that don't become projects: up to 24 months, then deleted.
- Client and project records: for the length of our relationship and as required for tax and legal purposes.
- Security data used for rate limiting: up to one hour.
- Analytics data: according to the retention period set in Google Analytics.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- access the personal information we hold about you
- correct inaccurate information
- ask us to delete your information
- object to or restrict certain processing
- receive your information in a portable format
- withdraw consent at any time, for example by changing your cookie settings
- complain to a data protection authority

**United Kingdom and European Union.** These rights apply under the UK GDPR and EU GDPR. In the UK you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

**Australia.** We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988. You can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

**Singapore.** We handle personal data in line with the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). You can withdraw consent or ask for access and correction at any time.

**United States.** Residents of states with privacy laws, such as California (CCPA/CPRA), can ask to know, correct or delete their personal information, and we will not discriminate against you for doing so. We do not sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

**Sri Lanka.** We handle personal data in line with the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022.

To make a request, email info@webulexpro.com. We may need to confirm your identity before responding.

## Security

We protect your information with measures such as encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted access, secure hosting and keeping software up to date. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you and the relevant authorities about a breach where the law requires it.

## Children

Our services are for businesses, and our website is not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
