Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. This policy explains which cookies **webulexpro.com** uses and how you can control them.

## Your choices

When you first visit, we ask whether you accept analytics and marketing cookies. Essential cookies are always on because the site needs them. Analytics and marketing cookies are only set if you agree.

You can change your choice at any time with the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page, or by clearing cookies in your browser.

## Cookies we use

### Essential (always on)

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `wbx` | Keeps you signed in to a form session and protects our forms against misuse (CSRF protection). Only set on pages with a form. | Until you close your browser |
| `wbx_consent` | Remembers your cookie choices. | 12 months |
| Cloudflare Turnstile | Checks that a form is sent by a person, not a bot. Set only on pages with a form. | Session |

### Analytics (only with your consent)

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Google Analytics 4: shows us how visitors use the site, so we can improve it. | Up to 2 years |
